# Angular Material survey: plan executed

[Survey README](README.md) · [Full plan](inventory/PLAN.md)

The survey ran in two parts: a file, folder and export survey (steps 1–7), and a
capability research and proposal extension (steps 8–11). The full step definitions are
in the [original plan](inventory/PLAN.md#execution-plan).
