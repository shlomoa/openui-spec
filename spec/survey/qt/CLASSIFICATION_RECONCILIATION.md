## Split, merge and reuse decisions

- Split component surface, response, trigger, state and visual feedback semantically; preserve original four-aspect descriptions as source evidence.
- Merge modal restriction/focus into one proposed leaf while keeping B04/B06 as distinguishable response aspects. Merge base, kinetic and reveal scrolling into one leaf while retaining B21/B22/B23 definitions.
- Reuse existing collapse/resize/drag-and-drop scopes; do not generate a leaf for each affordance, gesture or action.
- Keep B09, B11 and B18 as coordinated capabilities of Choice controls, Graphics viewport and Page stack/Tabs, respectively.
- Reuse Text completion, Exclusive selection coordination, Kinetic scrolling and Drag and drop taxonomy rows for their corresponding responses; add only the remaining 36 labels.
- All 58 prior added terms retain a classification record in [the term ledger](BEHAVIOR_TERM_CLASSIFICATION.md); all 40 outcomes map to destinations in [the response mapping](BEHAVIOR_SCOPE_MAPPING.md).

The 40 configuration/state and 15 appearance records are a normalized proposal vocabulary, not an exhaustive count of all possible Qt properties or themes. Their explicit units prevent presenting source classes, aliases and settings as a single inflated component total.
