# Proposed evidence for the Qt mapping

This is a candidate supplement to the [canonical evidence register](../../../scopes/evidence.md), not an approval ledger. Steps 10–14 have supplied neutral decisions and contracts; these remain proposed until canonical acceptance. The canonical register remains the source of truth for approved leaf evidence. The user's request authorizes preparing this proposal, not promotion of every framework capability into a normative contract.

Source kind for the Qt material is **external framework example**. Survey abstract names and crosswalk destinations are **proposed interpretations**. Normalized contracts, accessibility obligations and new taxonomy aliases are **proposed explicit decisions**, not already approved sources. Each row below links the observed examples and limits what they can support.
