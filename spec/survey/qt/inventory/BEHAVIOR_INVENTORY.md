# Behavior inventory — steps 7 and 8

Status: audit and concept separation completed for all 94 original entries (99 Qt names) and all 58 proposed taxonomy additions. This is a review of the researched descriptions with targeted public-source checks, not a fresh exhaustive member-level review of Qt. Original component assignments and totals remain the historical baseline.

Every original entry below has an explicit reviewed outcome, including entries from which no additional behavior is extracted. Source links retain the documented configuration and application-provided limits. The 58 additions are separately audited in [proposed-term classification](BEHAVIOR_TERM_CLASSIFICATION.md); they are aliases/variants of this research, not 58 newly discovered Qt objects.

## Classification rules

- Components own or present UI content; parts/affordances have an owning component.
- Behaviors describe observable actions, restrictions or transitions. The 40 behavior IDs are research concepts, not 40 proposed scope leaves.
- Triggers/gestures and state conditions initiate or qualify a response. They are not complete behaviors by themselves.
- Configuration/state records settings and current values; appearance depicts them. A setting can enable a behavior without becoming its definition.
- Layout mechanisms and scene composition retain their existing conceptual kind; they are not forced into a component/behavior binary.
- Only concept-specific findings are expanded per row. Shared inherited QWidget lifecycle/focus capabilities are contextual, not repeated as mandatory capabilities of every Qt entry. A None extracted result is not a claim that the object has no inherited or application-added behavior.

The source's purpose/appearance/contents research remains in the linked catalog. Behavior findings below reference [the proposed hierarchy](BEHAVIOR_TAXONOMY_PROPOSAL.md). They are evidence-level associations; the [completed contracts](BEHAVIOR_CONTRACTS.md) and [applicability matrix](COMPONENT_BEHAVIOR_MATRIX.md) now provide the final proposal relationships.
