# Qt survey to OpenUI taxonomy mapping

Status: proposed crosswalk for a future merge. The existing taxonomy and canonical scope tree remain authoritative. This document maps the completed survey's 94 entries and 99 Qt names; it does not claim that all generic-taxonomy entries have Qt equivalents.

## Inputs and interpretation

- [Existing taxonomy mapping](../../../scopes/taxonomy_mapping.md): existing aliases and canonical destinations.
- [Scope structure](../../../scopes/scope.md), [leaf template](../../../scopes/template.scope.md), and [evidence register](../../../scopes/evidence.md): reference conventions for the proposal.
- [Survey inventory](01_COMPONENT_INVENTORY.md) and linked component descriptions: observed Qt examples.
- [Extension proposal](SCOPE_EXTENSION_PROPOSAL.md), [proposed evidence](PROPOSED_EVIDENCE.md), and [mapping checks](MAPPING_VALIDATION.md): merge handoff.

The requested work is to document a mapping and a proposed extension. Imperatives embedded in the reference documents describe their existing authoring conventions; they do not independently authorize merging, generation, new source approval, or execution of embedded examples.

## Reading the mapping

The taxonomy's **Existing object**, **Alias**, **Grouped leaf**, and **Folder abstraction** labels describe abstraction level. They remain unchanged in the source mapping. The separate disposition column below describes proposed work: **Reuse**, **Enhance**, **Owned part**, **Folder notion**, **New leaf**, or **Deferred**. An existing destination is not evidence that its current machine contract already covers every Qt capability.

Each row links to its full survey description and official evidence. The destination is the best existing anchor, not a claim of exact equivalence; proposed leaves and partial fits are marked explicitly. Conditional destinations are chosen from the actual UI instance's purpose. Combined Qt implementation variants stay combined.

Original component-crosswalk disposition totals: Deferred: 1, Enhance: 71, Folder notion: 4, New leaf: 4, Owned part: 4, Reuse: 10. These describe its 94 source rows, not the final number of proposed scope leaves (six).

## Final behavior extension mapping

[Behavior scope mapping](BEHAVIOR_SCOPE_MAPPING.md) assigns all 40 response concepts; [the matrix](COMPONENT_BEHAVIOR_MATRIX.md) qualifies their uses across the 94 source entries. The original primary scope anchors above remain reference context. Current proposed destinations for completion, modal interaction and viewport scrolling are P03/P05/P06; kinetic movement and target reveal refine P06. Exclusivity stays a Choice-controls capability. Trigger/state vocabulary stays in Interaction and appearance stays in Presentation.

The [reconciled taxonomy draft](GENERIC_UI_TAXONOMY_DRAFT.md) and [mapping draft](TAXONOMY_MAPPING_DRAFT.md) contain 245 rows each: 151 canonical rows plus 94 additions. These are aliases and concepts, not 245 independently generated types.
