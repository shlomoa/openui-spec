## Final proposal disposition

[Behavior scope mapping](BEHAVIOR_SCOPE_MAPPING.md) resolves candidate leaf status. Exclusivity stays a Choice-controls capability; completion uses P03; kinetic scrolling refines P06. Component variants remain source concepts, not duplicate behavior definitions.
