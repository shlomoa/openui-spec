## O2 Standalone and Controlling element labels

The survey's [terminology proposal](inventory/TERMINOLOGY_PROPOSAL.md#the-terms) labels each class as either standalone or acting on another element it references. It is a survey proposal; nothing was approved at survey level. The labels are used in the [scopes proposal](scopes_proposal.md#clusters): Filter bar, Value help and Personalization panels act on another element; Metadata-driven field has no label; the other seven clusters stand alone.

Consolidated outcome: Controlling element is an approved glossary term ([terminology A3](../terminology.md#41-glossary-terms)); Standalone is not added because it is the default ([terminology: Not added](../terminology.md#not-added)).

## O3 261 unclustered classes

What was done: after step B5, the leftovers of all libraries were pooled. Step B6 moved classes that fit an existing spec object back to Matched (for example the `sap.html.*` family to Native), moved non-UI framework classes to Out of scope, and grouped the rest into the 11 clusters of O1 where at least a few classes shared a base class and purpose. The remaining classes had no clear siblings and were not reviewed one by one. They are the `phaseB_leftover_pool.json` records whose `b6_cluster` is empty. The survey's draft notes give earlier counts (B6 draft: 263 to 270); the raw data after step B9 gives 261.

## O4 The four folder-abstraction scopes

Four of the eleven top-level scopes hold mechanisms, not concrete objects: their taxonomy entries are Folder abstractions. The survey's table has one row per OpenUI5 class, and OpenUI5 provides these mechanisms as modules and services rather than classes, so the survey left them out ([PLAN.md §10](inventory/PLAN.md#10-categories-with-no-concrete-objects)). Surveying them needs a table with one row per module or mechanism.

Legacy libraries (`sap.ui.commons`, `sap.ui.ux3`) and the Web Component wrappers (`sap.ui.webc.*`) were left out by the survey plan ([PLAN.md](PLAN.md#libraries)). Their classes are not in the raw data.
