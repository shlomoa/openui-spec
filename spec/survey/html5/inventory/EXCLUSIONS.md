# Exclusions and open decisions

[Baseline](BASELINE.md) · [Coverage](COVERAGE.md)

## Explicit exclusions from object-level research

- External specification contents: dependency links only, as required by step 1.3.
- WHATWG repository source/build files and unrelated libraries: the requested survey source is the published HTML specification.
- Unnamed algorithm steps, local variables, example markup instances, and arbitrary prose terms: not separate object candidates unless explicitly defined or indexed.
- Acknowledgments and intellectual-property material: retained in page/section coverage as supporting material, not proposed as UI objects.

## Open semantic decisions

- **Obsolete-feature research:** step 1.4 was removed. This inventory retains chapter 16 and its 348 definition occurrences to keep coverage auditable, but does not reinstate a requirement to research them. Inclusion in later semantic research remains undecided. Legacy definitions elsewhere are not inferred to be excluded merely from their names.
- **Untyped definitions:** 4750 occurrences lack an explicit definition-type label. Their primary category is assigned by section; distinguishing algorithms, concepts, and internal bookkeeping requires step 5 research.
- **No standalone definition anchor:** 353 occurrences are linked to their enclosing section and listed below. They have structural ownership but need review before assigning canonical object identities. Some are explanatory examples or duplicate occurrences.
- **Abstraction:** no abstract-name equivalence is assumed for objects with similar names. This is deferred to step 4.
