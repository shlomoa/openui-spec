# Category map

[Baseline](BASELINE.md) · [Coverage](COVERAGE.md)

Categories follow the specification’s numbered chapters. Immediate numbered subsections are subcategories; deeper sections inherit their closest second-level subsection. Objects inherit their defining section’s primary category. This is an editorial research taxonomy, not HTML’s normative content-category classification. A chapter-level definition belongs to the direct-object table. Supporting appendices use a separate administrative category.

Canonical identity is the definition anchor, not its display name. Owner metadata is retained for members and scoped attributes. Distinct definitions with identical text are not merged. Definition occurrences without anchors use their containing section and remain flagged for semantic review.
